Support

Help with AyahFlow, and how to tell us about a problem.

Contact

Email buildwithabdallah@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, the iOS version and the app version (Settings → About in AyahFlow). There is no account and the app sends nothing to us, so we only see what you write.

Something wrong in a Quran text, du'a or narration?

Every ayah card has a report button that prepares a message with the exact reference. Texts are taken unchanged from their cited sources (Tanzil for the Quran; the sources named on each adhkar card), so please give the reference and what you expected.

Common questions

A Hijri date or holiday is a day off for me. Dates follow the Umm al-Qura calendar, and local moon sighting may differ. Settings → Hijri date correction moves every Hijri date by one day either way; holidays, Ramadan and their reminders follow.

Prayer times look early or late. Save your Home place, then choose the calculation method used where you live in the prayer settings.

How do I move to a new phone? Settings → Your data & privacy → Back up everything saves a file; on the new phone, use Restore progress with that file. Voice recordings and downloaded athan files are not in the backup.

Notifications or the athan don't come. Check that notifications are allowed for AyahFlow in iOS Settings, and that Focus or Silent mode isn't hiding them.

Is the Tajweed colouring a check of my recitation? No. It only colours the text, from machine-made annotations that have not been reviewed by a teacher.
