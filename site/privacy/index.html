Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026. Applies to the AyahFlow app on iPhone and Android and to its web preview.

In short

  • AyahFlow has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own.
  • Everything you do in the app is stored on your device only.
  • The app connects to the internet only for two things you turn on yourself: online recitation audio, and downloading an athan recording.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: practice answers, adhkar you marked as recited, prayer check-ins, counts, bookmarks, highlights, notes, your Quran plan and settings.
  • Places you save (Home, Work, mosque) and the moments the app detected, used for prayer times, the Qibla and place reminders.
  • Recitations you record with the microphone. They are never uploaded.
  • What the widgets show (prayer times, today's counts and adhkar you confirmed, the next Friday and fasts, the du'a of the day) is shared with them through the device's keychain, not over the network.
  • AyahFlow does not count steps or keep any fitness data. Earlier versions could show the phone's step count; that feature has been removed and its settings are deleted when you update.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this from the device. Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may include AyahFlow's data, under your account and settings, as it does for other apps; AyahFlow has no access to it. Settings → Your data & privacy can save a copy to a file you choose where to keep, or delete everything:

  • Back up everything saves all your AyahFlow data, your saved places included, so you can restore it later. Keep that file somewhere private: it contains your saved places.
  • Export my progress leaves out your saved places and the moments the app detected.
  • Neither file includes your voice recordings or downloaded athan recordings, which stay on the device. Nothing is uploaded: the file goes only where you send it.

Permissions, and why

PermissionUsed for
LocationSaving your places, calculating prayer times and the Qibla. With "Always", reminding you of the fitting dhikr when you leave or arrive at a saved place. Coordinates stay on the device.
MotionOnly if you turn on the riding reminder: noticing a car journey for the riding du'a. Nothing about your movement is stored; motion data stays on the device.
MicrophoneRecording your own recitation, only when you tap record. Nothing is uploaded. (Following your place while you recite is not included in this version.)
NotificationsReminders, the athan and prayer check-ins you choose.
Alarms (iPhone, iOS 26 and later)The Fajr alarm, if you turn it on.

When the app uses the internet

  • Online recitation audio, off until you turn it on in Settings: the recitation of an ayah is streamed from Islamic Network's audio service (cdn.islamic.network). That service sees your device's internet address and which ayah is requested, like any website. See its terms.
  • More athan reciters, only when you tap Download: the recording is downloaded once from AlAdhan (cdn.aladhan.com) and then kept on the device.
  • Links to Sunnah.com, Quran.com, Tanzil and others open in your browser only when you tap them.

The Quran text, translation, Tajweed colouring, adhkar and narrations, the hadith library, the 99 Names, and the athan recordings bundled with the app are all inside the app and need no connection. Greeting cards and the Zakat calculator work on the device; a card leaves the phone only if you share it, and what you type in the calculator is not saved.

What AyahFlow does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers or crash reporting services.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none leaves your device.
  • No AI service or model is called over the network.

Children

AyahFlow collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts sending anything off the device, this page will say so before that version is released, and the app will ask first.
