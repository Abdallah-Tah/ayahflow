AyahFlow

Quran practice, adhkar and prayer times on your iPhone, in English, French and Arabic.

  • Read the Quran (Tanzil Uthmani text) in Mushaf, Easy or Verse layouts, with optional Tajweed colours.
  • Practise and review ayahs with spaced review.
  • Morning, evening and daily adhkar, each quoted from its cited narration.
  • Prayer times, the athan, the Qibla and a Fajr alarm.
  • Islamic occasions, Ramadan and Eid on the Umm al-Qura calendar (local sighting may differ by a day).
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.
